About

I am a second-year physics PhD student at Yale University, advised by Prof. Konrad Lehnert. I work on Josephson junction circuits, and my work spans the whole experimental loop: device design, nanofabrication, millikelvin measurement, and modeling.

Before Yale, I received my B.S. in Physics from ShanghaiTech University and spent a year at UC Berkeley in Prof. Michael F. Crommie's group, where I grew single-layer kagome metal–organic frameworks on gate-tunable van der Waals devices and studied them by scanning tunneling microscopy.

Research

Josephson junction arrays in engineered microwave environments

2025 – present

Yale University · Advisor: Prof. Konrad Lehnert

Fabricating Josephson junction arrays and measuring their transport at millikelvin temperatures, to understand how the on-chip environment affects their behavior.

Phase dynamics of small Josephson junctions

2025 – present

Yale University · Advisor: Prof. Konrad Lehnert

Studying how the high-frequency surroundings of a small junction shape its phase dynamics, combining nanofabrication with low-temperature measurement.

Gate-tunable single-layer kagome metal–organic frameworks

2024

UC Berkeley · Mentor: Prof. Michael F. Crommie

Grew single-layer Ni3(HITP)2 frameworks on Au(111), HOPG, and WSe2, built graphene and WSe2 field-effect devices as gate-tunable substrates, and characterized the kagome-derived electronic structure with low-temperature STM and STS.

arXiv

Graphene break-junction devices

2024

UC Berkeley · Mentor: Prof. Michael F. Crommie

Built graphene-on-hBN devices with AFM-cut constrictions and shadow-mask gold contacts, and automated an electrical-breakdown procedure to form nanometer-scale gaps in the graphene channel.

Correlated electronic states in kagome metals

2022 – 2025

ShanghaiTech University · Advisor: Prof. Gang Li

Undergraduate thesis: a six-orbital tight-binding model of the AV3Sb5 kagome metals with fluctuation-exchange calculations of spin and charge fluctuations and the electronic instabilities they drive. Earlier work covered entanglement entropy in quantum spin liquids.
